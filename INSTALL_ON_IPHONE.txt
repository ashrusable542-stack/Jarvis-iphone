JARVIS iPhone Edition — Quick install

1) Publish this folder to a free HTTPS static host. Easiest free route:
   GitHub Pages. Follow README.md.
2) Open the published HTTPS URL in Safari on your iPhone 17.
3) Share -> Add to Home Screen.
4) Enable Open as Web App -> Add.
5) Launch JARVIS from your Home Screen.

This is an installable PWA, not an App Store native app.
The app has offline local features; full AI answers require opening ChatGPT separately.
